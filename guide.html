For New Players

Your first visit to Afterlight

You don't need to understand everything. Just walk, and see what you find.

What is Afterlight?

Afterlight is a quiet, anonymous multiplayer world. You appear as a small glowing orb drifting through a night sky shared with other real people. You can't see their names. They can't see yours.

The only thing you can do here is leave something — and find something left by someone else.

When you leave a message, it becomes a meteor: a glowing object floating in the world. Anyone who passes by can read it. If they choose to acknowledge it, the meteor transforms permanently into a star. You — the person who wrote it — receive a single quiet notification:

What you see
✦   Someone heard you.

That's the whole point. Something you were carrying alone, even briefly, was witnessed by another person.

What you see when you open the game

The screen fills with a dark night sky — subtle stars, a faint grid, the soft glow of the world's edge. Your player is the small bright white-blue orb at the center.

Other players appear as soft lavender orbs, moving in real time. You won't know who they are.

Scattered around you are golden glowing circles — these are the places where you can release a meteor. Walk toward one and it will brighten as you get close.

What you'll see on screen
  ○  — lavender orb (another player)
  ●  — bright white orb (you)
  ◎  — golden dashed circle (creation zone — press E when near)
  ✦  — soft colored glow (a meteor someone left)
  ·   — small bright point (a star — a meteor that was acknowledged)

The world is large. Take your time. There's no timer, no score, no penalty for exploring slowly.

How to share a meteor

Meteors are how you speak in Afterlight. Your message is anonymous — no name, no profile, just words.

1
Walk to a golden circle

These are scattered around the world from where you start. Walk toward one — it will brighten as you get close. A prompt appears: Press E to create meteor.

2
Press E to open the form

A small panel appears over the game. The world keeps running underneath — other players can still move while you write.

3
Choose a category

Pick the one that fits what you're carrying. It doesn't need to be exact.

Burden

Something heavy. A worry, a fear, a thing you've been holding.

Moment

Something that happened. A memory, an observation, a fragment of a day.

Hope

Something you're reaching toward. A wish, a possibility, a small belief.

Gratitude

Something you're glad exists. A person, a feeling, a quiet thing.

4
Write your message (up to 280 characters)

Write whatever feels true. It doesn't need to be polished. No one will know it was you.

5
Click Release

The form closes. A glowing meteor — colored by category — appears in the world at your position. Every connected player can now see it and walk toward it.

Your message is anonymous. No username, no avatar name, no profile is attached to it. Other players see only the category and the words.

How to acknowledge someone else's meteor

When you find a meteor, you can choose to respond to it. This is the most meaningful action in Afterlight.

1
Walk toward a colored glow

Meteors appear as small glowing orbs — red for Burden, cyan for Moment, green for Hope, gold for Gratitude. When you're close, a prompt appears: Press E to read.

2
Press E to read it

A panel shows the category and the message. Take a moment to read it.

3
Choose how to respond

Five responses are available. Pick the one that feels honest.

  • I feel this too.
  • You're not alone.
  • I hope things get lighter.
  • Take it one day at a time.
  • I'm glad you shared this.

Or close the panel with Pass by — you don't have to respond to everything.

4
The meteor transforms

The colored glow disappears. A small golden star appears in its place — permanently. Every connected player sees this happen in real time.

What happens after an acknowledgment

The writer sees

A notification appears at the top of their screen: "Someone heard you." They don't know who. They don't need to.

You receive Light

A small ✦ 1 Light counter appears in your bottom-right corner. Light is earned by witnessing others — it will be used in future stages to grow a personal space.

One acknowledgment is enough. The first response transforms a meteor into a star. The writer's message has been heard — that's complete. You cannot acknowledge your own meteor, and you cannot acknowledge the same meteor twice.

What is Light?

Light is a quiet resource you earn by acknowledging other players' meteors. Every time you witness something another person shared — and choose to respond — you earn ✦ 1 Light.

Light is not a score. It won't be shown to other players. There's no leaderboard, no rank, no badge for earning the most. It's simply a record of moments when you showed up for someone.

In a future stage, Light will be used to grow your own personal space inside the world — a small garden you can shape and add to over time.

What are stars?

Stars are permanent. Once a meteor becomes a star, it stays in the world. Every player who joins — now or in the future — will see it when they pass through that part of the sky.

A star means: this was said, and someone heard it. That's what it records. Not who said it, not who heard it — just the fact that the exchange happened.

Over time, the world fills with stars. The night sky you're walking through was shaped by real people who were here before you.

A few things to know

  • You're anonymous to everyone, including the server team. There are no accounts, no profiles, no login. You are identified only by a temporary session token stored in your browser.
  • Each browser tab is a separate player. If you open two tabs, they are treated as two different people. This is intentional — it lets you test the full loop locally without needing a second device.
  • Refreshing the page reconnects you to the same anonymous session. Your identity persists within the same browser tab until you close it.
  • Content is not editable or deletable once released, in the current stage. Safety hardening — including a report flow — is planned for Stage 5 before any public release.
  • You can pass by any meteor. Acknowledging is a choice, not a requirement. Walking past something is fine.

What Afterlight is not

Afterlight is not a mental health service. It is not therapy, crisis counseling, or a substitute for professional care. It does not provide advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you or someone you know is in crisis, please reach out to a qualified professional or a crisis line in your region.

Afterlight is a game about gentle, anonymous expression — the kind of thing you might write in a notebook, or say quietly to yourself. The "Someone heard you" moment is real, but it comes from another anonymous player, not a trained counselor.

Content that describes immediate risk to life is blocked by the server before it ever becomes visible in the world.
